RESEARCH OVERVIEW

My research focuses on the development of advanced Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Artificial Intelligence models to solve complex real-world problems across multiple domains such as healthcare, cybersecurity, agriculture, education, and intelligent systems. I aim to design smart, reliable, and efficient solutions that support high-impact applications with accuracy, safety, and scalability.

Current and future research interests include:

  • Developing hybrid machine learning and deep learning models for nonlinear and complex systems
  • Designing trustworthy and explainable AI systems with robustness and safety verification
  • Applying AI techniques in cybersecurity, digital forensics, threat detection, and privacy protection
  • Building intelligent healthcare systems for medical diagnosis using imaging, signal processing, and multimodal data
  • Creating real-time, lightweight, and open-source AI solutions for robotics, automation, and smart environments
  • Exploring Natural Language Processing, Generative AI, and multimodal learning for emerging applications

Recognition: Listed among the World's Top 2% Scientists in 2022, 2023, 2024, and 2025 (Stanford University and Scopus rankings).

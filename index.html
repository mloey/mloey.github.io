I am Mohamed Loey, an Associate Professor in the Computer Science Department within the Faculty of Computers and Artificial Intelligence at Benha University, Egypt.

My research focuses on the development of advanced Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning models to solve complex real-world problems. Specifically, I specialize in designing smart and reliable solutions using Machine Learning and Deep Learning architectures, including Generative AI and multimodal learning.

My research interests include:

  • Medical image analysis for building intelligent healthcare diagnosis systems using imaging and signal processing.
  • Cybersecurity and digital forensics, focusing on threat detection, privacy protection, and secure network usage.
  • Computer vision applications, such as face mask detection and plant disease identification.
  • Natural Language Processing and speech recognition, including the classification of cough sound symptoms and generating images from Arabic text.
  • Performance optimization and modeling of nonlinear and complex systems using simulation tools and hybrid algorithms.

I have been recognized for my global research impact by being listed among the World's Top 2% Scientists for four consecutive years: 2022, 2023, 2024, and 2025, according to rankings released by Stanford University and Scopus.

Education and Experience

Mohamed Loey is currently an Associate Professor in the Computer Science Department at the Faculty of Computers and Artificial Intelligence, Benha University, Egypt. He is presently an Assistant Professor seconded to the College of Engineering at A'Sharqiyah University, Oman, where he serves in the Cybersecurity program. Previously, he served as a full-time faculty member at Buraydah Private Colleges in Saudi Arabia from 2022 to 2024, where he serves in the Cybersecurity program.

In 2021, he served as the Head and Coordinator of the Information Technology Program at the Industry and Energy Technology College, New Cairo Technological University, Egypt. He earned his Ph.D. in Computer Science from Benha University in 2017. He received his M.Sc. in Information Technology in 2010 and his B.Sc. in Information Technology in 2006, both from the Faculty of Computers and Artificial Intelligence at Cairo University. Earlier in his career, starting in September 2008, he served as a Demonstrator and Assistant Lecturer at Sinai University.

Publication and Peer Review

Mohamed Loey has published over 40 publications with over 5,060 citations. He has authored high-quality research papers in prestigious journals, including Artificial Intelligence Review, Computers in Biology and Medicine, Sustainable Cities and Society, Neural Computing and Applications, Cognitive Computation, Measurement, Symmetry, and Expert Systems.

His global research impact is further evidenced by his inclusion in the World's Top 2% Scientists list by Stanford University and Scopus for four consecutive years: 2022, 2023, 2024, and 2025. Additionally, he serves as a reviewer for more than 20 international journals, including IEEE Access, PLOS ONE, Cluster Computing, Scientific Reports, IEEE Transactions on Affective Computing, and Signal, Image and Video Processing.
